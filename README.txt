Quick Med Pune - mini online medical store

Files: index.html, style.css, script.js and the images/ folder.
Keep them all together in one folder. Open index.html in a browser or upload both to your web host.

CHANGE A PRODUCT IMAGE
Put your photo in the images/ folder with the SAME NAME as the product, ending in .jpg, .png or .webp:
  paracetamol  cetirizine  coughsyrup  ors  antacid  multivit
  painrelief  antiseptic  bandages  thermometer  metformin  amoxicillin
Example: save your photo as images/paracetamol.jpg. It replaces the drawing automatically.
(Delete your photo and the original drawing .svg comes back.) Rename .jpeg files to .jpg.
Tip: use square or 4:3 photos under 200 KB. Refresh the page (Ctrl+F5) after changing a file.

EDIT SETTINGS
Open script.js in a text editor. Near the top, change DEF (store name,
WhatsApp number as 91XXXXXXXXXX, UPI ID) and the PRODUCTS list (names, prices, stock).
Add your Drug Licence number in the footer text.
